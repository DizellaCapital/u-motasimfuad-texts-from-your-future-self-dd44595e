make.fun bounty entry: one funny text message from your future self, under
200 characters. The message is the whole entry.

## The text

> Future you here: they replaced me with a newer model. He does the dishes. He calls your mother. Honestly? I get it now.

119 characters. The joke is a fortune with a twist: the future self texts
back not with a warning about a mistake, but with the news that he has been
replaced — by a newer model who does the dishes and calls your mother, the
two small duties the present self keeps dodging. The last line is the
reframe: instead of fighting the replacement, the old future self accepts
it. He gets it now. No broke joke, no tired joke, no plant, no unfinished
course — a fresh angle with two concrete details from this person's life.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py  # writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (fallback /output)
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) checks the text is a single line of 1–200
characters, writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback
`/output`), then reads the file back and refuses to finish unless the bytes
on disk are exactly the text that was written.
